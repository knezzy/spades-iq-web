Common questions

Can I play without Wi-Fi?

Yes. The current release is built for offline play. Game Center features and analytics may require an internet connection.

Does the app show ads or offer purchases?

The current release does not show ads or offer in-app purchases.

How do I request information about my data?

Email us using the address on this page and describe your request. Please do not send a password or payment card information.

Need a hand?

For gameplay, settings, or privacy help, email us. Include your device model, iOS version, and a short description if you are reporting a technical problem.
