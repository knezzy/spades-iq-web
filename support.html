Common questions

Can I play without Wi-Fi?

Yes. Solo games can be played offline. Live tables, online rankings, and related features require an internet connection.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the in-app store or purchase settings and use Restore Purchases. Sign in with the same Apple account used for the original purchase. Consumable items such as coin packs generally do not restore through Apple's restore flow.

How do I change ad tracking permission?

Open iOS Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking. You can also review any advertising consent options available inside the app.

How do I request account data or deletion?

Email us using the address on this page. Include the display name associated with your account and describe your request. Please do not send a password or payment card information.

Need a hand?

For gameplay, live table, purchase, or privacy help, email us. Include your device model, iOS version, and a short description if you are reporting a technical problem.
