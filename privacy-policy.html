Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you use the Spades IQ app and this website.

Last updated October 12, 2026

This policy covers the current Spades IQ iPhone release and the Spades IQ website. The current release focuses on offline games. Online accounts, ads, and in-app purchases are disabled in this release.

Information processed in the app

  • Gameplay and settings. Game progress, results, statistics, and preferences may be stored on your device so offline features work.
  • Game Center. If you use Game Center, Apple may process your player identifier, achievements, leaderboard scores, and related activity. Some Game Center information may be visible to other players under Apple's settings.
  • Analytics and diagnostics. Production builds use Firebase Analytics to process app usage events and identifiers. Google and Apple may also process device, app, IP address, crash, and performance information needed to deliver their services and help us improve the game.
  • Support information. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include.

Offline game data generally remains on your device. Game Center and analytics use network services when available.

Analytics, ads, and purchases

We use Firebase Analytics to understand app use and improve gameplay. Firebase Remote Config may deliver app configuration. The current release does not show ads, request ad tracking permission, or offer in-app purchases. If those practices change, we will update this policy. See Google's privacy policy for information about Google's services.

Apple services

The app uses Apple services such as Game Center for achievements and leaderboards. Apple processes information under its own terms and policies. See Apple's privacy information.

How we use and share information

We use information to provide gameplay, maintain achievements and leaderboards, respond to support, and improve the app. Information may be shared with Apple and Google for the services described above. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the service and its users. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and your choices

Local game data remains on your device until you remove it or the app. Apple and Google retain data under their own policies. Support emails are kept as long as needed to respond and maintain necessary records. You can manage Game Center through your Apple account. For questions or requests about information we control, email us below. We may need to verify your request and may retain information where required or permitted.

This website

This static website does not include its own analytics, ads, account forms, or tracking scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver the pages. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Spades IQ is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The current version and its update date will be posted here. For privacy questions, requests, or support, contact kyleknez4@gmail.com.
