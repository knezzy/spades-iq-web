Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you use the Spades IQ app and this website.

Last updated October 12, 2026

This policy covers Spades IQ (also called Spades with Friends in some technical components) and the Spades IQ website. The app includes offline play and may offer online games, account features, purchases, analytics, and advertising. The features you use affect what information is processed.

Information processed in the app

  • Account and profile information. If you use live features, we may process an account identifier, your chosen display name and avatar, and information supplied through a sign-in provider, such as an email address when available.
  • Gameplay and social information. We may process bids, plays, results, rankings, achievements, tournament and table activity, invitations, relationships, and messages you send in live games. Profile and gameplay details may be visible to other players where the feature calls for it.
  • Purchases and entitlements. The app may process product IDs, transaction IDs, purchase status, balances, and entitlement information to provide and restore digital items. Apple handles payment details; we do not receive your full card number.
  • Device, usage, and diagnostic information. The app and its service providers may process app interactions, device or app identifiers, IP address, crash information, and performance data to operate, secure, measure, and improve the experience.
  • Support information. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include.

Offline game progress and preferences may be stored on your device. Live play requires information to be sent to our online service so games, profiles, and related features can work across devices and players.

Advertising and analytics

Spades IQ includes Google AdMob and Firebase. Where enabled, AdMob may show rewarded or interstitial ads and process information such as device or advertising identifiers (when permitted), IP address, and ad interactions to deliver, measure, and limit ads. Firebase Analytics may process app usage events and identifiers to help us understand and improve the game. Firebase Remote Config may help control app features. Ad availability can vary by version, region, and entitlement.

Where applicable, the app may present an advertising consent form or ask for permission using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings. Declining tracking does not necessarily remove all ads.

Learn more from Google's advertising information and Google's privacy policy.

Apple services

The app may use Apple services including Sign in with Apple, Game Center, and StoreKit for identity, achievements, leaderboards, and purchases. Apple processes information under its own terms and policies. See Apple's privacy information.

How we use and share information

We use information to run games and accounts, show profiles and rankings, deliver purchases and rewards, respond to support, prevent abuse, and improve the app. We share information with service providers when needed for those purposes, including Apple and Google services described above. Information you choose to post or share in live features may be seen by other players. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the service and its users. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and your choices

Information is kept for as long as needed to provide the relevant feature, maintain records such as purchase and game history, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. You can stop using online features, control tracking in iOS Settings, and manage App Store purchases through your Apple account. To request access to or deletion of information associated with your Spades IQ account, email us at the address below. We may need to verify your request and may retain information where required or permitted.

This website

This static website does not include its own analytics, ads, account forms, or tracking scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver the pages. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Spades IQ is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The current version and its update date will be posted here. For privacy questions, requests, or support, contact kyleknez4@gmail.com.
